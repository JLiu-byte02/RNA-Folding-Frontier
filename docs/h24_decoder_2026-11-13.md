## What this does to Amendment 13 section A13.3

A13.3 pre-registers, for `L < 300` at the pair-budget point, that decoder A
"reduces long-range false positives and increases true positives" relative to
greedy nested decoding, with `test_novel` as the clean test and the withheld
bins exploratory.

- **On withheld the rule passes in all three bins.** The 150-299 reduction is
  small in relative terms (-148 of 14,528 = -1.0%), not a qualitative change.
- **On id_test the rule fails in two of three bins.** `L<80` loses 41 true
  positives, and 150-299 gains 125 long-range false positives (+14.5%).

Therefore the subgroup claim is **split-dependent**, not a property of the
method. It remains pre-registered and will be evaluated on `test_novel` as one
item of the Amendment 14 one-shot list, but no report may describe a general
`L<300` improvement unless that confirmatory run passes. The robust,
reproduced statement is narrower: **in the 80-149 nt bin decoder A gains true
positives and does not increase long-range false positives on both splits.**
