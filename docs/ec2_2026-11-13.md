# E-C2 coverage gate — result (A13.2) — 2026-11-13

Status: **A13.2 fails.** The frozen selector does not beat a constant global
scale by the pre-registered margin, and on `id_test` it is significantly worse.
The E-C2 line is closed as a *performance* claim. One implementation incident
(O(n^2) decoding) is recorded in section 6 because it cost ~61 CPU-h.

All numbers are v3 labels, nested truth, per-sequence exclusion below 4 true
pairs, family-block bootstrap (2,000 resamples, seed 2026..), one seed (17) for
the head. No two splits are ever pooled.

## 1. What was pre-registered

`experiments/001_e1prime/AMENDMENT_13.md` (sha `b3a87a58...`). Target: the
coverage gate must beat the *better external baseline* by >= .02 with a 95% CI
excluding zero, on withheld, without losing that gain on `id_test`.

Gate family, applied as a rescale of the whole score matrix (offset b = 0):

```
G1: s(x) = s_max * sigmoid(alpha - beta * z(pppl) - gamma * log L)
G2: s(x) = s_max * sigmoid(alpha - beta * z(pppl) - gamma * log L)
```

fit on the train dump, 81-point grid, objective = mean over 5 clan-grouped
folds of family-macro F1, one parameter set chosen per gate family, then frozen.
z is computed from frozen train pppl (mean 15.334844, sd 256.832739). The fit is
`experiments/002_e1prime_runs/ec2_fit.json`, sha256
`7008310f93478c308f17c4b709b75abf27a065377bb83a06887a176b25202490`, 16,558
sequences (702 dropped for <4 pairs), 5,822 s wall on 12 workers.

## 2. The fit landed on the `beta = 0` boundary

| gate | s_max | alpha | beta | gamma | folds (family-macro F1) | mean | pooled |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| G1 | 0.1875 | 0.0 | **0.0** | 0.0 | .7202 .6156 .6165 .6164 .6377 | 0.6413 (sd .0404) | 0.6259 |
| G2 | 1.0 | -1.0 | **0.0** | 0.25 | .7179 .6157 .6178 .6173 .6378 | 0.6413 (sd .0392) | 0.6264 |

Both gates drive the confidence weight to zero. G1 then collapses to
`s = 0.1875` for every sequence — a **constant scale**, i.e. no gate at all. G2
varies only with length. The grid constrained `beta >= 0`, so a *negative*
dependence of scale on uncertainty is **untested, not excluded**; that is a
protocol limitation and must be stated as such if this line is ever revived.

The absolute cross-validated F1 here (≈.64) is optimistic: the score matrices
come from a head trained on all folds, so the CV selects the gate without
holding out the head.

## 3. Withheld confirmatory read — FAIL

16,163 sequences / 409 families. 630 dropped for missing pppl, 0 for <4 pairs
(the dump was already filtered).

|---|---:|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| RNAfold (reference) | 0.6003 | [0.5739, 0.6275] | 0.5548 | 284021 / 317917 / 137883 | 37.24 |
| fixed s = .1875 | 0.6137 | [0.5879, 0.6405] | 0.6103 | 304996 / 272514 / 116908 | 35.73 |
| G1 | 0.6168 | [0.5908, 0.6432] | 0.6038 | 305153 / 283740 / 116751 | 36.43 |
| G2 | 0.6161 | [0.5903, 0.6426] | 0.6033 | 304876 / 283901 / 117028 | 36.43 |

Paired family-block deltas:

| comparison | delta | 95% CI | reads |
|---|---:|---|---|
| G1 - fixed | +0.0031 | [-0.0038, +0.0088] | n.s. |
| G2 - fixed | +0.0024 | [-0.0028, +0.0073] | n.s. |

So: the *constant scale* beats RNAfold, and the fitted gate adds nothing on top
of it (needs >= .02, observed +.003 with a CI spanning zero). Against the true
best withheld baseline, EternaFold at **0.6051**, G1's margin is only **+0.0117**
— below the .02 requirement and, per the pre-registration, not a pass either way.

**A13.2 verdict: FAIL.**

## 5. Scale-confound control — G1 is literally the constant arm

Because `beta = 0`, G1 emits one scale for every sequence. Re-running the
withheld read with the fixed arm at G1's scale (0.09375 = 3/32) makes G1 and
fixed **bit-identical**:

| arm | family-macro F1 | micro F1 | TP / FP / FN |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| fixed s = .09375 | 0.6168 | 0.6038 | 305153 / 283740 / 116751 |
| G1 | 0.6168 | 0.6038 | 305153 / 283740 / 116751 |
| G2 | 0.6161 | 0.6033 | 304876 / 283901 / 117028 |

`G2 - fixed = -0.0007 [-0.0036, +0.0037]`. This is the decisive diagnostic: the
entire E-C2 "gain over the baseline" is a **global score-scale effect**, and the
learned confidence signal contributes exactly zero. Any future gate must be
tested against a scale-matched constant arm by construction, not after the fact.

## 6. Implementation incident: O(n^2) decoding, ~61 CPU-h

`scripts/score_ec2_gate.py` originally decoded every item once per arm scale
instead of once per item. Job 15627 ran 3:50:08 wall on 16 allocated cores
(≈61 CPU-h) before cancellation. The 298-item smoke test passed because the
squared cost is invisible at that size.

Fix: `_init` now enforces `len(scales) == len(items)` and records
`decodes_per_item: 1`; a regression test
(`tests/test_scoring.py::test_ec2_gate_decodes_each_sequence_once`) pins it, and
the gate script prints `arm <name>: N decodes (n items x 1)` plus a 1,908-item
medium timing self-check before the confirmatory read.

Linear cost afterwards: withheld 3 arms x 16,163 items = 48,489 decodes in
**3:46 wall on 16 cores (≈1.0 CPU-h)**; the `id_test` job (3 arms on 1,287 +
3 arms on 16,163) took **11:42 on 8 cores (≈1.6 CPU-h)**.

Job 15761 exited non-zero although it had already written the withheld artifact:
its trailing `id_test` step ran before `ec2_pppl_id_test.json` existed, printed
`no usable records`, and `set -euo pipefail` propagated the failure. The
withheld artifact is valid; the `id_test` read was moved to its own sbatch
(`scripts/jobs/ec2_idtest.sbatch`, job 15765).

## 7. What this does and does not rule out

Ruled out:

- a *positive* confidence-weighted coverage gate of this parametric form, fitted
  on train pppl, as a source of family-macro F1 gains (constant scale dominates);
- any claim that the E-C2 margin reflects uncertainty awareness.

Not ruled out:

- a *negative* dependence of scale on pppl (`beta < 0` was outside the grid);
- per-family or per-length-band scale selection, which is a different estimator
  and would need its own pre-registration and its own scale-matched control;
- the underlying observation that the head is over-predicting pairs relative to
  v3 truth (mean pred pairs 45.6-47.0 vs a median truth of 24), which is a
  property of the readout, not of the gate.
