## H3: evolutionarily consistent context transfers

Common a/c/e population: 8,476 sequences, 93 families.

| quantity | point | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| `R_c = (c-e)/(a-e)` | .8947 | [.7263, 1.0517] |
| `R_d = (d-e)/(a-e)` | .2255 | [.0188, .3425] |

The pre-registered support rule required `R_c` CI lower bound ≥ .90 and `R_d`
CI upper bound ≤ .30. **H3 is not supported.** The point estimates are
directionally consistent with homolog context transferring better than
other-clan context, but the c interval crosses the bar and the d interval
crosses .30.

The exploratory identity bins were `.876` [.276, 3.425] for 0.40–0.50
(545 sequences, 32 families) and `.897` [.729, 1.056] for 0.50–0.60
(7,931 sequences, 93 families); the direction is not usable as evidence at
these sample sizes.

On id_test the common a/c/e population is 526 sequences / 30 families:
`R_c=.625 [.296,.820]`, `R_d=−.036 [−.412,.210]`. No H3 verdict is taken on
id_test; the numbers are descriptive and directionally consistent with a
weaker version of the same transfer, not a confirmation.

## P3 train-and-test and the D1-core verdict

All arms use the same 16,163 withheld sequences and 409 families. The a arm was
retrained under Amendment 11 so that its fit/validation split (`16,716/544`)
matches both block arms.

| arm | family-macro F1 | 95% CI | mean micro F1 |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| a native | .5959 | [.5726, .6191] | .5968 |
| e e-block (≈ k=32 crop) | .5965 | [.5733, .6196] | .5852 |
| b b-block | .5860 | [.5630, .6088] | .5766 |

Paired family deltas versus a:

| arm | seed-averaged Δ | 95% CI | per-seed Δ |
|---|---:|---|---|
| e | +.0007 | [−.0079, +.0093] | −.0090, +.0064, +.0046 |
| b | −.0099 | [−.0159, −.0036] | −.0215, −.0019, −.0061 |

**D1-core is falsified.** Neither e-block- nor b-block-trained heads beat the
native-trained head by the pre-registered .02 with a CI excluding zero. The
e-block arm is statistically indistinguishable from a; the b-block arm is
slightly worse. The nested-subset primary readout gives the same ordering
(a .5951, e .5956, b .5847).

This is a train-and-test result, not a test-time swap: it says that removing
distal context during training does not improve OOD performance under this
intervention. It does not prove that the FM contains no family signal; the FM
was pretrained on RNAcentral, and e-block/b-block also change the input
distribution. Those limits were recorded in Amendment 10 before the result.

## Frozen (s, b) and H2.4

The coarse grid selected `s=.25, b=0`, the lower s edge, so Amendment 7 A7.4
refinement was run. The refined grid selected **s=.1875, b=0** (interior;
mean fold F1 .6059). The fit JSON has sha256
`be54ddab6552102b645a42dff5ac60eaa92072680db062ae437c8c4889ad5ad5`
and is recorded in `state/amendments.lock.json`.

H2.4 used p2fix seed17, v3 primary truth, and the frozen calibration. No
threshold on the 25-point grid reached A-energy's recall, so both splits used
the pre-registered pair-budget fallback.

| split | greedy pairs | DP pairs | greedy TP | DP TP | greedy LR-FP | DP LR-FP | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| id_test | 58,636 | 58,649 | 28,965 | 29,835 | 3,284 | 4,205 | not confirmed |
| withheld | 580,562 | 577,510 | 284,906 | 306,046 | 17,630 | 20,218 | not confirmed |

Energy-consistent decoding gains true positives at the matched budget but
increases long-range false positives on both splits. **H2.4 is not
confirmed**; the pre-registered verdict required fewer long-range FPs and at
least as many TPs.
